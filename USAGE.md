# Usage Guide

## Quick Start

1. **Open the Application**
   - Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser
   - No installation or setup required!

2. **Search for Packages**
   - Select your departure city (Delhi or Chandigarh)
   - Choose your travel dates (Dec 20-25 or Jan 02-08)
   - Pick trip duration (2 or 3 days)
   - Click "Search Packages"

3. **Review Results**
   - Browse available packages sorted by price
   - Each package shows:
     - Flight details (airline, timing, duration, price)
     - Hotel details (name, rating, type, per-night price)
     - Total package price

## Understanding the Results

### Flight Information
- **Airline**: Carrier operating the flight
- **Departure**: Departure time from origin
- **Duration**: Total flight time
- **Type**: DIRECT (non-stop flights only)
- **Price**: One-way price (total shows × 2 for round trip)

### Hotel Information
- **Hotel**: Name of the property
- **Rating**: 5-star properties only (⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐)
- **Type**: Hotel or Resort
- **Nights**: Number of nights (duration - 1 day)
- **Per Night**: Nightly rate

### Total Price
The total package price includes:
- Round-trip flights (2 × flight price)
- Hotel stay (per night × number of nights)
- All prices in INR (Indian Rupees)

## Tips

1. **December vs January**: December prices are typically higher (peak holiday season)
2. **2 vs 3 Days**: 3-day trips include 2 nights of accommodation
3. **Compare Options**: Results are sorted by total price (lowest first)
4. **Book Early**: These are sample prices; actual prices may vary

## Troubleshooting

**Q: No results showing?**
- Check that you've selected all three options (city, dates, duration)
- Wait 2-3 seconds for results to load

**Q: Fewer results from Chandigarh?**
- This is expected - Chandigarh has fewer direct flight routes than Delhi

**Q: Prices seem high?**
- December is peak holiday season with premium pricing
- All properties are 5-star luxury hotels/resorts
